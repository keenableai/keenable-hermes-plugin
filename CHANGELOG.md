# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Skills: pass `objective` only for multi-step or ambiguous questions, say what the query leaves out and what earlier searches found, and keep personal details out of it. The data-flow section lists `objective` among what Keenable receives.

## 1.0.0

- First release: Keenable's hosted MCP server (`search_web_pages`, `fetch_page_content`)
  as a keyless portable connection, the `keenable-search` and `keenable-research`
  skills, and an optional API-key entry for `config.yaml`.
